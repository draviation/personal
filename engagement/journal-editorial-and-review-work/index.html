Journal editorial & review

Editorial leadership and service to the scholarly community.

I have served as the Editor for Collegiate Aviation Review International (CARI), a peer reviewed journal of the University Aviation Association (UAA) during 2021 – 2023. The CARI welcomes the following types of manuscripts: Peer-Reviewed Articles, Peer-reviewed Practices, Position Papers, Literature Reviews, Editorials, and UAA Conference Proceedings.
